## Project Abstract

The Finance Project is a Java-based budgeting application designed for busy college students who need a simple way to manage their finances. The application will allow users to create a monthly budget, organize their spending into customizable categories, and track their expenses throughout the month. Users will be able to monitor their spending and see how much of their budget remains in each category. At the beginning of a new month, the application will refresh the budget so users can begin tracking their spending for the new month. The goal of the project is to provide college students with a convenient and easy-to-use tool for managing their money while balancing their busy schedules.
